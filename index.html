Hi, I'm Ganga.

Data Science & Engineering student · University of Connecticut (Honors Program) · Class of 2028

Most of what I do comes back to the same question: how do you take a pile of messy data and actually get something useful out of it. Lately that's meant building machine learning models, working with microbiome sequencing data as an undergraduate researcher, and building full-stack applications from scratch.

My work spans NLP, statistical modeling, and software engineering, and I like projects that force me to connect theory with something people can actually use — from a marketplace app for farmers and buyers to an academic advising chatbot. Outside of coding, I write for UConn's student science journal, mentor first-year students, and help run events for the Data Science Club.
